Tips

Type <what> : <when>. Before the colon, up to three comma-separated parts in any order: a masterplan task, a free name, and/or the word Event. Examples: Growth : mon 10-11am, Growth, Fred : mon 10-11am (shown “Growth (Fred)”), Growth, Fred, Event : mon 10-11am (event on the task), Fred, Event : mon 10-11am (plain event), chairs : mon 10-11am (one-off). Days: every day (Mon–Sat), mon-fri, tue and thu, weekends (Sat). Weeks: this week, whole masterplan, or weeks 38-51. Start with delete to bulk-remove matching blocks. Drag either end of a block to resize; tap a block for nudge buttons. The notes in the side bar: coloured tabs on top (click the open tab to rename / recolour / delete it, + for a new note). Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic, Ctrl+H pink, Ctrl+Shift+8 bullets, Ctrl+Shift+9 tick boxes, Tab / Shift+Tab indent. A bullet with sub-bullets shows ▾ — click it (or Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓) to fold / unfold. Ticked items move to the bottom of the dump note; click one there to untick it. Move a line (with its sub-items): drag the ⠿ grip at its right end (drag left below the last sub-item to take it a level up), or Alt+Shift+↑ / ↓, or the ↑ ↓ buttons. Aa marks the line at the cursor: priority colours 1–5 (Alt+1…5, Alt+0 = none), bold (Alt+B), highlight (Alt+H), italics (Alt+I). Undo / redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) — with the cursor outside the note; inside the note they undo / redo your typing.
